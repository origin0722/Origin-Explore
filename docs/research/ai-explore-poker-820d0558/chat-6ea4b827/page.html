Explore — AI-Powered Structured Thinking & Knowledge Exploration Tool

Explore is an application that enables deep conversations with your favorite AI. Here, you can break free from the constraints of linear chat boxes and easily achieve multi-level dialogues. Complex discussions that used to get lost in linear conversations can now be fully unfolded here.

Core Features

Smart Annotation

  • Click on underlined text to quickly preview its explanation.

Hierarchical Dialogue

  • Click the new card button in different directions to create corresponding cards for hierarchical dialogue.
  • Downstream cards automatically read relevant information from upstream cards.
    • ↗: Sub-card
      • Reads the corresponding title of the upstream card as the background theme.
    • →: Divergent Card
      • Reads the corresponding title of the upstream card as the related theme.
    • ↓: Branch Card
      • Inherits the theme of the upstream card and the conversation history before the branch point.

Document Reading

  • Import documents for efficient reading and exploration.

Thought Universe

  • Express your understanding of knowledge in your own words, and the AI will integrate it into your Thought Universe.

Context Management

  • A more free, precise, and intuitive way to manage conversation context.
  • Select AI response text to quote it.
    • Multiple quotes supported.

Immersive Interface & Interaction

  • Meticulously designed conversational interface interactions.
  • Unread indicators on card tree navigation nodes; right-click to manually toggle.

Smart Summary

  • Smart summarization of project content available in the Favorites section.

Personalization

  • Choose your language and color theme to help you enter a flow state.
